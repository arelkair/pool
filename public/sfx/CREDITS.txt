All sounds and the music in this folder are original, synthesized for this game, with no third-party material.

ball-hit-1.ogg, ball-hit-2.ogg, rail-hit.ogg, pocket.ogg, cue-strike.ogg
  Filtered noise and decaying sine bursts.

notify.ogg, turn-chime.ogg, win.ogg, lose.ogg
  Soft electric-piano style tones in short phrases.

music-loop.ogg
  Electric-piano loop in D minor (Dm9 - Bbmaj7 - Cmaj7 - Gm6), 72 bpm, 8 bars.

The interface click is synthesized at runtime and has no file.
