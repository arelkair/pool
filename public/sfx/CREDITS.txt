All sounds and the music in this folder are original, synthesized for this game, with no third-party material.

ball-hit-1.ogg to ball-hit-4.ogg
  Ball-on-ball clicks: a noise transient plus damped inharmonic modes, four variants.

cue-strike.ogg
  Leather tip contact plus a low knock.

rail-hit.ogg
  Cushion thud: low-passed noise and damped low modes.

pocket.ogg
  Jaw click, rolling rumble, drop and thud.

notify.ogg, turn-chime.ogg, win.ogg, lose.ogg
  Bell tones with inharmonic partials in short phrases.

music-loop.ogg
  Pad, plucked arpeggios and soft bass over Fmaj9 - Am7 - Bbmaj7 - Cmaj7 - Fmaj9 - Dm9 - Bbmaj9 - Cadd9, 76 bpm, 8 bars.

The interface click is synthesized at runtime and has no file.
